## Problem Title
Farm2Door – AI-Powered Direct Agricultural Marketplace

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Farm2Door is an AI-powered digital platform that connects farmers directly with consumers, retailers and wholesale buyers by reducing the involvement of unnecessary middlemen. Farmers can register on the platform, upload their available agricultural products, mention quantities and set their prices. Consumers can browse products, compare prices and purchase fresh agricultural products directly from farmers. The platform also includes AI-based demand forecasting, smart price recommendations and delivery route optimization to improve agricultural marketing and distribution.
1.2 How does the solution address the problem?
Farm2Door addresses the problem of multiple intermediaries by creating a direct connection between farmers and buyers. It reduces unnecessary commissions, improves price transparency and helps farmers receive better returns for their products. Consumers can purchase fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices. The integrated logistics system also helps improve transportation and reduce delays.
1.3 Innovation and uniqueness
- Direct farmer-to-consumer communication and transactions.
- AI-based demand forecasting to predict future product requirements.
- Smart price recommendations based on market trends.
- Multilingual support for farmers in regional languages.
- Route optimization for efficient product delivery.
- Group purchasing to combine orders from nearby consumers.

## Technical Approach
The Farm2Door platform will be developed using modern web technologies and artificial intelligence tools.
- Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
- Backend: Python Flask.
- Database: MySQL.
- Machine Learning: Python and Scikit-learn.
- Data Visualization: Chart.js.
- Mapping: OpenStreetMap.
- Development Environment: Visual Studio Code and GitHub.
These technologies will provide a user-friendly, scalable and efficient platform.
2.2 Methodology and implementation
Paste this:

The implementation begins with user registration, where farmers and buyers create their accounts. Farmers can upload product details, availability, quantity and prices. Buyers can browse the marketplace and place orders. The backend processes the orders and stores information in the database. AI algorithms analyze previous sales and market trends to predict product demand and recommend suitable prices. Finally, the logistics module helps arrange deliveries and identify efficient transportation routes.
2.3 Prototype details
Paste this:

The proposed prototype consists of the following modules:
1. Farmer Dashboard – Allows farmers to register, upload products and manage orders.
2. Consumer Dashboard – Allows consumers to browse products and purchase them.
3. Product Marketplace – Displays available agricultural products and their prices.
4. AI Prediction Module – Predicts product demand using historical data.
5. Logistics Module – Helps manage deliveries and optimize routes.
6. Admin Dashboard – Allows administrators to manage users, products and transactions.
## Feasibility and Viability
Farm2Door is technically and economically feasible because it uses easily accessible technologies such as Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript and MySQL. Open-source tools reduce development costs. The platform can be accessed through smartphones and computers, making it convenient for farmers and consumers. It can initially be implemented in a small region and expanded to larger agricultural markets in the future.
3.2 Potential challenges and risks
- Limited digital knowledge among some farmers.
- Poor internet connectivity in rural areas.
- Transportation and delivery difficulties.
- Changes in product availability and market prices.
- Inaccurate AI predictions due to insufficient historical data.
3.3 Strategies to overcome challenges
- Provide a simple and easy-to-use interface.
- Include regional language support.
- Allow farmers to update product availability regularly.
- Collaborate with local delivery partners and farmer organizations.
- Improve AI predictions using updated market and sales data.

## Impact and Benefits
Farm2Door benefits farmers by providing direct access to consumers and wholesale buyers, improving their earning opportunities and reducing dependency on intermediaries. Consumers benefit from fresh agricultural products at reasonable prices. Retailers and restaurants can purchase products directly from farmers, ensuring a more organized and reliable supply chain.
4.2 Benefits of the solution
- Economic Benefits: Better earning opportunities for farmers and reduced unnecessary commissions.
- Social Benefits: Stronger connections between rural farmers and urban consumers.
- Environmental Benefits: Reduced unnecessary transportation through route optimization.
- Agricultural Benefits: Better planning through AI-based demand forecasting.
- Consumer Benefits: Access to fresh products with transparent pricing.
- Technological Benefits: Encourages digital transformation in agriculture.
